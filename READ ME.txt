SOMETHING ELSE THAT IS VERY IMPORTANT- THE LAN Launcher APP AUTOMATICALY DOWNLOADS THE @CF MOD AND @VPPAdminTools MOD WHEN YOU CLICK THE INSTALL BUTTON. EVERY SO OFTEN, EITHER THE @VPPAdminTools MOD or the @CF MOD CAN AND WILL BE UPDATED IN THE STEAM WORKSHOP. WHEN THIS HAPPENS AND WHEN YOU ARE USING THE RECENT-OUTDATED VERSION OF EITHER ONE OF THESE TWO MODS AND THEN WHEN YOU LAUNCH ANY ONE OF SERVER-MAPS, YOU WILL NOT BE ABLE TO LOG INTO YOUR LAN SERVER.

SO TO FIX THIS PROBLEM THIS IS WHAT YOU WILL HAVE TO DO, YOU WILL HAVE TO OPEN YOUR FILE EXPLORER AND CLICK Windows (C:), THEN YOU CLICK Program Files (x86), THEN 
CLICK THE Steam FOLDER, THEN CLICK THE steamapps FOLDER, THEN CLICK THE common FOLDER. IN THE common FOLDER YOU WILL FIND A FOLDER CALLED DayZServer (YOUR LAN SERVER).
THIS IS WHERE YOU WANT TO PASTE THE UPDATED-NEWER VERSION OF THE MOD. 

GO INTO STEAM AND CLICK WORKSHOP, THEN WHEN YOU GET TO THE WORKSHOP, TYPE IN THE NAME OF THE MOD, @CF or @VPPAdminTools AND UNSUBSCRIBE TO IT. NOW YOU MUST 
SUBSCRIBE TO IT AGAIN. 

THEN YOU GO TO YOUR STEAM LAUNCHER AND CLICK WHERE IT SAYS -MODS-. SEARCH THROUGH THAT UNTIL YOU FIND THE @CF or VPPAdminTools. THEN YOU CLICK THE LITTLE ARROW OF THE SELECTED MOD, NOW CLICK THE LITTLE 3 DOTS. YOU WILL SEE WHERE IT SAYS -OPEN FOLDER IN WINDOWS EXPLORER, CLICK THAT AND THEN FIND THE UPDATED MOD, THEN RIGHT CLICK IT AND COPY.

NOW GO BACK INTO YOUR DayZServer BASE FOLDER AND PASTE THE UPDATED MOD INTO THAT FOLDER AND YOUR DONE! //
