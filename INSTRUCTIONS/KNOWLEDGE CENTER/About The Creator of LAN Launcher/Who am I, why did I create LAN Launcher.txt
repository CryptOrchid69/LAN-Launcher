ABOUT THE KNOWLEDGE CENTER

When you go into the Knowledge Center, you will find some information that you may think is questionable. There is information about changing Hardware ID and IPv4 public address. 

Usually, whenever someone changes the HWID and IPv4 public address it is because they are trying to dodge a GAME BAN. This sort of thing would certainly make you 
think that by placing this kind of information in the Knowledge Center, that I am helping Cheaters. 

I want to make something very clear, I did not put this information in the Knowledge Center to help CHEATERS manipulate anti cheats. I placed this information in the 
Knowledge Center to help VICTIMS of hackers and faulty anti cheats, such as BattlEye. 

Let me explain myself more clearly. Almost two years ago, I was playing DayZ which uses the anti cheat known as BattlEye. After two years of playing DayZ, something very strange had happened. For absolutely no comprehensible reason, I had received a notification from BattlEye that I was Globally Banned from DayZ. I had also received a notification from STEAM that I was VAC banned.

My heart dropped all the down to my ass, I sat there in front of my PC with my eyes wide open not knowing what to think. Whenever any anti cheat places a GLOBAL BAN on your gaming account, you will not only be banned from that specific game, you will also be banned from any other games that uses that very same anti cheat.

If you were globally banned by BattlEye in DayZ, then you will be banned from other games like GTA 5 online, Rust, etc., 

Until this very day, I still have absolutely no idea of how I could have been banned. I had absolutely no suspicious cheat scripts or programs sitting on my desktop. I had no suspicious apps of any kind sitting on my desktop and I know for certainty, that I wasn't cheating. Could it have been some hacker who had found a way to 
manipulate BattlEye to get me banned?
